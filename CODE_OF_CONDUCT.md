# Code of conduct

Teum is built in public with a high bar for respectful, evidence-led collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Critique the work, not the person.
- Explain claims with a reproduction, source, screenshot, or concrete product case when possible.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy, consent, accessibility needs, and responsible disclosure.
- Accept a maintainer decision once the tradeoff and next step are recorded.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing private vulnerability details are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone represents the project elsewhere. Once the canonical repository exists, report conduct concerns privately to its maintainers and security concerns through `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers may edit or remove content, pause participation, or ban a contributor when needed to protect the community.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid exposing the reporter.
